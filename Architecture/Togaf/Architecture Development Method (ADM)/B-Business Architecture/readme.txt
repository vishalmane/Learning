Business Architecture (TOGAF ADM Phase B)

🔹 Purpose
To develop the Target Business Architecture that describes how the enterprise needs to operate to meet the business goals,
respond to strategic drivers, and serve its stakeholders.

🎯 Objectives
Develop the baseline and target Business Architecture.
Define the business functions, processes, roles, actors, and organizational structure.
Align business architecture with strategic goals and drivers.
Identify business priorities and constraints.
Identify and validate business requirements for IT support.

🎯 Goals
Establish a clear view of how business functions are executed and how they interact.
Ensure alignment between business and IT.
Provide input to Data, Application, and Technology Architectures.
Support stakeholder concerns and requirements.

📌 Key Steps
Step	Activity
1	Select reference models, viewpoints, and tools (e.g., Business Reference Models, ArchiMate)
2	Develop baseline Business Architecture (current state)
3	Develop target Business Architecture (future state)
4	Perform gap analysis (between baseline and target)
5	Define business architecture components (processes, actors, roles, services, organizations)
6	Validate with stakeholders
7	Identify business transformation initiatives
8	Assess impact and dependencies
9	Update architecture repository
10	Generate Business Architecture artifacts for use in next ADM phases

🧾 Outputs
Baseline and Target Business Architecture models
Business process models and value streams
Organization charts and role definitions
Business Function and Service Catalogs
Gap analysis results
Business Requirement Document
Updated Architecture Definition Document (ADD)
Updated Architecture Requirements Specification (ARS)
Architecture Roadmap components
Architecture Views (e.g., value chains, capability maps)
